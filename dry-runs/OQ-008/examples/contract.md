# Definitions {#definitions}

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into
as of the date of last signature below (the [Effective Date]{.def}) by and
between Acme Corp, a Delaware corporation (the [Company]{.def}), and Beta
LLC, a California limited liability company (the [Recipient]{.def}).

[Company]{.term} and [Recipient]{.term} are each a "Party" and together
the "Parties."

## Confidential Information {#confidential-information-def}

"Confidential Information" (the [Confidential Information]{.def}) means
any non-public information disclosed by either Party to the other,
whether in writing, orally, or by inspection, that is designated as
confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be
confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances
of disclosure.[^scope-note]

# Obligations of the Recipient {#obligations}

## Non-Disclosure {#non-disclosure}

The Recipient shall not disclose any [Confidential Information]{.term} of
the Disclosing Party to any third party without the Disclosing Party's
prior written consent, except as permitted under [[ref:permitted-disclosures]].

## Standard of Care {#standard-of-care}

The Recipient shall protect the Disclosing Party's [Confidential Information]{.term}
using at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own
confidential information of similar importance, and in no event less
than reasonable care.

### Permitted Disclosures {#permitted-disclosures}

The Recipient may disclose [Confidential Information]{.term} to its
employees, officers, and professional advisors who:

(a) have a need to know that information for the purpose of this
    Agreement; and

(b) are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as
    those in this Agreement.

#### Compelled Disclosure {#compelled-disclosure}

If the Recipient is compelled by law or a valid court order to disclose
any [Confidential Information]{.term}, it shall, to the extent legally
permitted, give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice before
disclosure so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order, as
further limited by [[ref:term-and-termination]].[^court-order-note]

# Exclusions {#exclusions}

[Confidential Information]{.term} does not include information that the
Recipient can demonstrate: was already known to it without an
obligation of confidentiality; is or becomes public through no fault of
the Recipient; is received from a third party without breach of any
confidentiality obligation; or is independently developed without use of
or reference to the Disclosing Party's [Confidential Information]{.term}.

# Term and Termination {#term-and-termination}

This Agreement shall remain in effect for two (2) years from the
[Effective Date]{.term}, provided that the confidentiality obligations
in [[ref:non-disclosure]] and [[ref:standard-of-care]] shall survive
termination for a period of five (5) years from the date of disclosure of
the relevant [Confidential Information]{.term}.

# Governing Law {#governing-law}

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware,
without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.
