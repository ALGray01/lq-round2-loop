1 Definitions

“Agreement” means this Master Services Agreement.

2 Confidential Information

Each party agrees to protect the other party’s Confidential Information, subject to the exclusions in sec. 2.1.

2.1 Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information already publicly available.

3 Amendment

This Agreement may only be amended by a written instrument signed by both parties, as provided in sec. 3 itself and cross-cited from sec. 1 above.
