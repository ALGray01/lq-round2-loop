\section{Definitions}\label{sec:definitions}

``Agreement'' means this Master Services Agreement.

\section{Confidential Information}\label{sec:confidential}

Each party agrees to protect the other party's Confidential Information,
subject to the exclusions in Section \ref{sec:exclusions}.

\subsection{Exclusions}\label{sec:exclusions}

Confidential Information does not include information already publicly
available.

\section{Amendment}\label{sec:amendment}

This Agreement may only be amended by a written instrument signed by
both parties, as provided in Section \ref{sec:amendment} itself and
cross-cited from Section \ref{sec:definitions} above.
