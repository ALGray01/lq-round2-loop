# Definitions {#sec-definitions}

"Agreement" means this Master Services Agreement.

# Confidential Information {#sec-confidential}

Each party agrees to protect the other party's Confidential Information,
subject to the exclusions in [Section 2.1](#sec-exclusions).

## Exclusions {#sec-exclusions}

Confidential Information does not include information already publicly
available.

# Amendment {#sec-amendment}

This Agreement may only be amended by a written instrument signed by both
parties, as cross-cited from [Section 1](#sec-definitions) above.
