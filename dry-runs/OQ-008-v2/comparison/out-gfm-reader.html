Definitions {#sec:definitions}

"Agreement" means this Master Services Agreement.

Confidential Information {#sec:confidential}

Each party agrees to protect the other party's Confidential Information, subject to the exclusions in Section \ref{sec:exclusions}.

Exclusions {#sec:exclusions}

Confidential Information does not include information already publicly available.

Amendment {#sec:amendment}

This Agreement may only be amended by a written instrument signed by both parties, as provided in Section \ref{sec:amendment} itself and cross-cited from Section \ref{sec:definitions} above.
