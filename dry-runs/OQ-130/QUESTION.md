QUESTION (OQ-130 — Friction-free delivery layer for interactive HTML legal explainers):
Lawyers now ship interactive HTML one-pagers — toggles, calculators, clause-by-clause commentary — and clients prefer them to Word. The format has won; delivery hasn't. Email won't render HTML, attachments break on a phone, public hosting leaks confidentiality. Build the missing layer: turn an HTML explainer into a per-client, access-controlled link that renders right on mobile, with token-level access and a way to revoke it. Prove it on two real explainers — a contract-penalty calculator and a multi-section memo — delivered to a simulated client on both desktop and phone; report where the content lives, who can reach it, and how a link dies. A link the client just clicks beats "download this attachment, don't open it on mobile, find it in your files."
