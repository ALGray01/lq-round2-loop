County in the Collony of Virginia Gentleman Deceased, Who was the Eldest Son & Heir at Law, & Also the only Acting Executor of Benjamin Bordin the
Elder, late of Orange County in the Collony of Virginia aforesd. Gentleman Deceased of the one part, And Jacob Gray, Plantationer of the said
